Step 1 of 2: connect PayPal (free practice account)

  1. Go to developer.paypal.com and log in with your PayPal login.
  2. Click Apps & Credentials.
  3. Make sure Sandbox is selected at the top (not Live).
  4. Click Create App, type any name, and click Create App again.
  5. Copy the Client ID, and the Secret (click Show), and paste them below.

Step 2 of 2: connect Claude

  1. Go to console.anthropic.com and sign in (or make a free account).
  2. Click API Keys, then Create Key.
  3. Copy the key and paste it below. If it asks for billing, add a few dollars of credit. This app uses a few cents.

Your keys are saved only on this computer.
